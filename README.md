# Humanitarians AI Research

**Every paper we are working on, and the papers already published.**

[How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Papers in progress](#papers-in-progress) · [Published work](#published-work)

**We're inviting collaborators.** Each paper in progress has its own public repository, where you can read the current draft, or the plan if there is no draft yet, and see what needs doing. To help with a paper, read its repository's README, pick one task, and email Nik Bear Brown at bear@bearbrown.co to be added to that repository. You don't need to review a whole paper to help. The steps are in [How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The research is led by Nik Bear Brown with Northeastern University students, Humanitarians AI fellows and outside collaborators.

## Keeping this list

- **One row per paper.** Keep different versions of one paper (a preprint and its journal version) together.
- **Say exactly where a paper stands:** in progress, submitted, under review, accepted, in production, published, preprint, declined or withdrawn. A conference program doesn't prove proceedings publication, and a preprint isn't peer-review acceptance.
- **Link, don't copy.** Link to the paper's repository, DOI or venue page. Never put manuscripts under review, reviews, decision letters, email or anyone's contact details in this repository.
- **Something missing or wrong?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/hai-research/issues/new) or email Nik.
